Newton's 2nd Law: Pulley & Free Body Diagram (FBD)

System: m₁ = 2.0 kg (table) · m₂ = 4.0 kg (hanging) · Frictionless pulley · g = 10 m/s²

AI WHITEBOARD TUTOR

Teaching Sequence

Physical System Setup — FBD for Block 1 — FBD for Block 2 — Newton's 2nd Law — Solve a & T

1. PHYSICAL SYSTEM & COORDINATE LAYOUT
We have a classic JEE mechanics system: Block 1 of mass 2 kg rests on a frictionless horizontal table and is attached via a light taut string over a frictionless pulley to hanging Block 2 of mass 4 kg.
2. FREE BODY DIAGRAM FOR BLOCK 1 (M1 ON TABLE)
Now we isolate Block 1 to draw its Free Body Diagram. Vertically, downward gravity m₁g is balanced by upward normal force N₁ from table. Horizontally, string tension T accelerates the block to the right.
3. FREE BODY DIAGRAM FOR BLOCK 2 (HANGING M2)
Next, we isolate the hanging mass m₂. Two collinear forces act vertically: gravity m₂g pulls downward with 40 Newtons, while tension T pulls upward. Because the block accelerates downward, m₂g - T = m₂a.
4. COUPLING EQUATIONS TO SOLVE
Equating both bodies: (1) T = m₁a = 2a and (2) m₂g - T = m₂a ⇒ 40 - T = 4a. Adding gives: 40 = 6a ⇒ a = 40/6 = 6.67 m/s². Tension T = 2(6.67) = 13.33 N.
5. FINAL VERIFICATION & CHECKPOINT
Check: Net accelerating force = m₂g = 40 N. Total mass accelerated = m₁ + m₂ = 6 kg. Acceleration a = F_net / M_total = 40 / 6 = 6.67 m/s². Tension T < m₂g confirms hanging mass accelerates down.
